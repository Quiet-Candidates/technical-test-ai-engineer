# AI log

This is the document we read first.

We are not checking whether you used AI. We assume you did. We want to see **how** — where you drove,
where you delegated, and where you caught it being wrong.

---

## 1. Tools

Which tools did you use, and for what? Be specific about the split — if one wrote the UI and another
wrote the tests, say so.

## 2. Your plan

What did you write down before you started building? Paste it as it was, including the parts you
later abandoned.

## 3. The prompts

Paste the prompts that mattered, in order. Not all of them — the ones that changed the direction of
the work.

If your tool can export the session, attach the export and point at the moments worth reading.

## 4. Where it was wrong

At least one concrete case: what the AI produced, why it was wrong, how you noticed, and what you did
about it.

If you never caught it being wrong in 24 hours, tell us how you were checking.

## 5. Your setup

Commit the configuration you wrote for your tools — `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, rules files, skills,
subagents, hooks, scripts. Then say here which of them actually earned their place.

## 6. What you kept for yourself

Which decisions did you refuse to delegate, and why those?

## 7. If you had a week

What part of this would you have automated instead of doing by hand?
